About Me

I am a Cloud Support Engineer at Amazon Web Services based in Cape Town, South Africa. I specialize in containers, Kubernetes, and DevOps tooling on AWS. I am a recognized Amazon EKS Subject Matter Expert (SME), helping enterprise customers design, deploy, and troubleshoot production Kubernetes workloads at scale.
